## The problem with a TV remote

Most smart TVs make you move across an on-screen keyboard one letter at a time. That is bearable for a short search word and miserable for a long link, an email address or a paragraph. The same pain applies to TV sticks, streaming boxes and game consoles with a built-in browser.

Sending text from your phone fixes this. You do the typing on a device made for it, and the TV only needs to receive six digits.

## What you need

- A TV, stick or console with a **web browser**. Look in the apps list for "Browser", "Internet" or "Web".
- A phone or computer to type on.
- Both devices connected to the internet. They do not have to be on the same network.

## Step by step

1. On your phone, paste or type the text, link or code into the **Send text** box.
2. Choose a short expiry. **10 minutes** is enough for a TV transfer.
3. Press **Save to clipboard** to see a 6-digit code.
4. On the TV, open the browser and go to this site. Use a bookmark next time so you do not have to type the address.
5. Select **Receive text** and enter the 6 digits with the number buttons on the remote.
6. The text appears on screen. For a link, you can type it into the address bar, or use the on-screen **Copy** button where the TV browser allows pasting.

## Common things to send

- **Long web addresses** for pages you want to see on a big screen, such as a recipe, a slideshow or a map.
- **Activation codes and device names** shown on one device and needed on another.
- **Meeting links** for a video call you want to see on the TV.
- **Subtitles or lyrics** copied from a page.
- **A message for guests,** such as a Wi-Fi network name and instructions, shown on the TV for a gathering. See [how to share a Wi-Fi password safely](/guides/share-wifi-password/) first.

## If the TV browser is limited

TV browsers vary a lot. Some points to keep in mind:

- **Pasting may not work.** If so, read the text off the screen and type it, which is still easier if the text is short.
- **Text can be small.** Use the TV browser's zoom, or send the content in short lines.
- **Some TV browsers are old.** If the page does not load, try another browser app, or use a streaming stick that has a better one.
- **Bookmarks help.** Save the site in the TV browser's favourites so it takes one click next time.

## Make it a one-tap routine

The first time takes a minute. After that it should take seconds.

1. **Bookmark the site** in the TV browser. Name the bookmark something short so it sits at the top of the list.
2. **Add the site to your phone's home screen** so it opens like an app.
3. **Keep expiry short.** Ten minutes means nothing lingers if you forget to clean up.
4. **Name things on the first line.** A heading such as `Recipe link` or `Quiz night rules` makes the clip obvious when it appears on the big screen.

## If something does not work

**The page loads but nothing happens when you press buttons.** Some TV browsers need you to move a cursor with the arrow keys and press OK. Look for a pointer mode in the browser menu.

**The text is cut off.** Ask for the text in shorter lines, or zoom out in the browser.

**The code is rejected.** Check you are on the **Receive text** tab and that you entered all six digits. If you used delete after the first view, the clip works only once, so make a new one.

**The page is blocked.** A few TVs restrict which sites open. Try another browser app, or use a computer or phone with a screen-sharing cable instead.

## Casting as an alternative

If your goal is to show a web page, photo or video on the TV, your phone may be able to cast directly to it, which avoids typing anything. Casting is great for media. The online clipboard is better when you need the *text* itself on the TV or its browser, such as a code or a long address.

## Privacy on a shared screen

A TV is a big screen in a room full of people. Before you send something:

- Do not send passwords or personal data.
- Turn on **Delete after the first view**, so the clip is removed as soon as the TV has opened it.
- Close the page afterwards, so the text is not left on screen.
- If the TV is in a hotel or rental, clear the browser data and log out of anything you opened.

## Quick tips

- Put your phone and the remote next to each other; you only need six digits.
- If you will be doing this often, add the site to your phone's home screen and bookmark it on the TV.
- Keep clips short-lived so nothing lingers on your account or the TV.
